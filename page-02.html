Architectural Positioning

The AB alongside SAP SRM / legacy SAP procurement — five domains that determine the boundary.

ClaraVis runs SAP S/4HANA with legacy SRM modules for procurement. SAP's built-in procurement automation handles catalogue purchasing and standard PO workflows within the SAP boundary. The first question any CPO or enterprise architect asks is: why build on GCP when SAP covers procurement? The answer is architectural, not commercial.

Canonical Positioning Statement

SAP SRM and standard S/4HANA procurement deliver automation within the SAP data model. The Autonomous Buyer delivers intelligence across the full P2P landscape — spanning the supplier ecosystem, the contract corpus, the AP platform, and the CSRD/LkSG compliance obligations that no single ERP boundary contains. Where SAP automates transactional procurement, the AB orchestrates the full Source-to-Pay-to-Compliance lifecycle, with explainability and human oversight engineered in to satisfy EU AI Act and GDPR Article 22 requirements that SAP's ML models cannot meet.

ADR-002 · GCP alongside SAP, not instead of it

DOMAIN — 01
AB on GCP — SAP has no access to this domain

Supplier intelligence across 1,400 external counterparties

Supplier risk data, CSRD compliance signals, LkSG due diligence records, and market intelligence about 1,400 suppliers do not live in SAP and never will. SAP SRM is a transactional platform, not a supplier intelligence fabric. Scoring supplier risk across financial, ESG, and delivery dimensions requires Vertex AI, BigQuery, and external data enrichment pipelines — none of which SAP provides with explainability or HITL by design.

DOMAIN — 02
AB on GCP — architectural requirement SAP cannot satisfy

EU AI Act Annex III & GDPR Art. 22 — explainability that satisfies the law

GDPR Article 22 prohibits fully automated decisions with significant effects on suppliers without a human in the loop. EU AI Act Annex III requires SHAP explanations per inference, written to an immutable audit log before any action commits. SAP's ML models are proprietary — feature attribution is not exposed to the operator. For a German medical OEM, this is a structural disqualification, not a configuration gap.

DOMAIN — 03
AB on GCP — cross-system orchestration outside SAP boundary

3-way match across SAP S/4HANA, SAP Ariba, and legacy AP

ClaraVis's 3-way match requires reconciling purchase orders from SAP S/4HANA, goods receipt notes from the warehouse system, and invoices arriving via the legacy AP platform and Ariba Network. These three data sources have no common event stream. SAP's standard matching operates within the ERP boundary. The AB is the orchestration layer that unifies them — with exception ML, tolerance-band logic, and HITL routing built in.

DOMAIN — 04
AB on GCP — document-native reasoning beyond ERP metadata

Full-document contract and SOW intelligence

Clause-level risk scoring, non-standard term detection, and supplier MSA analysis require reasoning across the full contract document. Gemini 1.5 Pro reads an entire supplier framework agreement in one pass via its 1M token context window. SAP Ariba CLM operates on structured contract metadata — not the source document. The gap is every non-standard clause that lives in unstructured text.

DOMAIN — 05
AB on GCP — purpose-built for cross-system P2P scope

Cross-system P2P orchestration — the layer nobody owns

ClaraVis's procurement landscape spans SAP Ariba (sourcing), SAP S/4HANA (ERP), a legacy AP platform, a supplier portal, a document management store, and a CSRD compliance register. No single vendor owns all of that. The AB is the connective tissue — a coherent, explainable, human-supervised orchestration layer across every system ClaraVis operates for procurement.

AI Readiness Assessment

Where ClaraVis Procurement stands today — five dimensions.

The AI Readiness Assessment is the input to the TOGAF Architecture Vision. It defines the starting position across five procurement-specific dimensions and frames the gap the AB is designed to close. Scored 1–5.

Spend Visibility
3/ 5

Ariba and SAP S/4HANA maintain structured spend data for strategic categories. The gap is the ~23% tail spend — fragmented across purchasing cards, direct requisitions, and non-PO invoices with no common classification. BigQuery spend fabric is not deployed.

Action: Unified Pub/Sub spend event pipeline + BigQuery spend fabric + tail spend classification model as AB Day 1 infrastructure.

Supplier Data Quality
2/ 5

Supplier master data is fragmented across Ariba, SAP, the supplier portal, and the compliance register. No golden supplier record exists. Deduplication is manual. 18% of supplier records have conflicting data across systems.

Action: Supplier master data unification is a prerequisite for the Supplier Risk Model and CSRD monitoring — blocks all supplier intelligence modules.

Process Automation Maturity
2/ 5

Ariba automates RFx publishing and basic approval routing. SAP handles standard PO workflow. Everything else — 3-way match, exception resolution, contract redlining, compliance checks, payment scheduling — is manual. Straight-through processing rate across the full P2P cycle is below 30%.

Action: 3-Way Match Agent and PO Agent are the highest-ROI Day 1 automations — they address the two largest manual effort concentrations.

Regulatory Posture
2/ 5

ISO 27001 and GDPR baseline compliance is established. AI-specific obligations under EU AI Act Annex III and GDPR Article 22 are not addressed for the two production ML models. CSRD/LkSG supplier monitoring is manual and 18 months behind. Q3 2025 audit flagged structural non-compliance across all three domains.

Action: XAI layer + HITL spec + Model Cards + CSRD continuous monitoring — all required before next compliance review. Blocks production deployment of any new ML model.

Org Change Readiness
3/ 5

CPO and CFO are active sponsors. The AP Manager team is enthusiastic about eliminating manual 3-way match. Legal and Compliance are cautious but engaged following the Q3 2025 audit. Category Management requires assurance that the Sourcing Agent enhances, not replaces, strategic sourcing discipline.

Action: HITL visibility and the SHAP explanation interface are the organisational unlock for Legal and Compliance. Demonstrate them first. Expand second.

Success Criteria

How ClaraVis Procurement will know the AB is working.

Observable architectural outcomes — not percentage projections. Each criterion is measurable at the system level and traceable to a specific requirement.

EU AI Act & GDPR Compliance
Every high-risk procurement inference is explainable and human-supervised
  • Every ML inference above a defined risk threshold produces a SHAP explanation written to the audit log before the downstream action executes
  • Every high-risk supplier decision routes through a named human approver via a formal HITL state — no exceptions, no bypasses
  • Every model in production has a versioned Model Card in Vertex AI Model Registry, reviewed and approved before promotion
  • A compliance audit query returns a complete, unbroken decision trail for any procurement inference made in the last 24 months
P2P Cycle Time
Every procurement handoff has a defined SLA and an immutable record
  • Every state transition in the P2P flow has a defined SLA — the agent tracks elapsed time and escalates automatically on breach
  • Every handoff produces an immutable audit record in Firestore before the next state is entered
  • Invoice cycle time reduced from 11 days to under 3 days for straight-through invoices
  • Ariba sourcing event status and SAP PO status synchronised via the AB event bus — no manual re-keying
3-Way Match Performance
Straight-through processing above 90% — exceptions routed, not queued
  • First-time match rate above 90% — measured against PO, GRN, and invoice data from the live Pub/Sub event stream
  • Every exception classified by the Match ML model with a confidence score before routing to HITL
  • Every tolerance-band approval written to the audit log with the rule that triggered it
  • Every HITL exception resolution actioned within the SLA defined in the AP Manager's HITL contract
CSRD / LkSG Compliance
1,400 suppliers monitored continuously — no annual questionnaire backlog
  • Every active supplier has a current compliance score updated on every new compliance signal
  • Every threshold breach triggers an automated HITL escalation to the Compliance Officer within the defined SLA
  • Every new supplier screened against CSRD, LkSG, and EUDR criteria before SAP vendor master activation
  • The compliance register is a query against the operational log — not a manually maintained spreadsheet
Data Sovereignty
No procurement data leaves the EU boundary — enforced at infrastructure layer
  • VPC-SC perimeter provisioned via Terraform — data residency constraint is in infrastructure code, not network policy
  • CMEK keys provisioned and owned by the ClaraVis Cloud KMS project
  • All GCP resources deployed to europe-west3 or europe-west4 — enforced by Organisation Policy constraint
Architecture Decision Records
Every significant design choice documented and traceable
  • Every module page links to the ADRs that govern its design decisions — no component exists without a traceable decision record
  • Every ADR states the alternatives considered and the reasoning for the choice made
  • The ADR index is maintained as a living document — updated when decisions change
ADR-001 · SAP Ariba Integration Pattern
Ariba REST API — sourcing system of record
Selected over direct SAP SRM API, CSV export, and mock data patterns
Ariba Network API integration was selected because the P2P domain depth of this portfolio is only demonstrable with a live system of record integration. A mock data pattern makes the sourcing intelligence claims unverifiable. Ariba Developer sandbox provides a free, permanent integration target with production-representative data structure.
Status: Accepted
ADR-002 · GCP alongside SAP Ariba and S/4HANA
Augmentation, not replacement
The AB addresses five domains outside SAP's boundary
The AB addresses five domains structurally outside SAP's capability: supplier ML intelligence with EU AI Act-compliant explainability, cross-system 3-way match across SAP and non-SAP AP, CSRD/LkSG continuous compliance monitoring, full-document contract intelligence, and cross-system P2P orchestration. SAP Ariba and S/4HANA remain the systems of record.
Status: Accepted
